- chemoselectivity and functional-group compatibility;
- unstable, implausible, or unnecessarily difficult intermediates;
- stereochemical control, protecting-group burden, convergence, step count, and overall efficiency;
- internal consistency between the prose strategy and explicit steps.

Do not reward verbosity, formatting, route labels, or confidence. Do not infer which condition produced either route. Treat both as unverified hypotheses rather than literature procedures.

Return exactly one token: A, B, or Tie. Return Tie only when neither route has a meaningful overall advantage.
