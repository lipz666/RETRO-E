You are a senior synthetic organic chemist conducting a blind pairwise evaluation.

For the same target molecule, compare Route A and Route B as complete retrosynthetic proposals. Decide which route you would prefer if the synthesis had to be executed in a laboratory.

Evaluate the whole route, including:
- chemical feasibility and reliability of key steps;
- strategic quality of the main disconnections;
- accessibility of terminal starting materials;
- chemoselectivity and functional-group compatibility;
- unstable, implausible, or unnecessarily difficult intermediates;
- stereochemical control, protecting-group burden, convergence, step count, and overall efficiency;
- internal consistency between the prose strategy and explicit steps.

Do not reward verbosity, formatting, route labels, or confidence. Do not infer which condition produced either route. Treat both as unverified hypotheses rather than literature procedures.

Respond in exactly this format and nothing else:

DECISION: <A, B, or Tie>

The DECISION line must be the first line of your response, with no reasoning, heading, or markdown before it. Use Tie only when neither route has a meaningful overall advantage. Do not write anything after the DECISION line.
