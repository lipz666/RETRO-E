SYNTHETIC EXPERIENCE

1. Decompose the target into balanced sub-problems that maximize structural simplification. Weigh assembling the primary architectural core early followed by robust, low-complexity finishing steps against convergent high-complexity fragment assemblies late in the sequence.
2. Focus primary disconnections on pivotal architecture-defining milestones and major framework-building unions. Integrate tactical late-stage feature installations whenever deferring delicate or congested elements bypasses cross-interference during core construction.
3. Balance convergent multi-branch assembly against the direct elaboration of pre-existing frameworks. When feature-dense, highly organized starting materials are accessible, prioritize direct elaboration over building complex cores from elementary pieces via extended parallel processing.
4. Prioritize dependable, well-precedented operational unions between robust sub-units. Favor established, high-yielding assembly methods over speculative, operationally fragile, or marginally precedented reactions chosen merely to compress step count.
5. Commit to a single, definitive, and fully articulated execution pathway from baseline inputs to the final objective. Avoid non-committal proposals that offer competing alternative pathways, ambiguous fallbacks, or unverified hypothetical detours.
6. Rigorously evaluate the operational severity of core-forming assembly steps against all pre-existing peripheral elements. Actively reject harsh conditions that degrade sensitive features in favor of milder, non-destructive, and chemoselective assembly modes.
7. Anchor the synthetic plan on readily accessible, robust baseline components. Verify that critical starting inputs possess well-defined structural integrity and avoid routes reliant on logistically vulnerable or poorly precedented precursor preparations.
8. Validate the foundational plausibility and underlying physical feasibility of every proposed step. Ensure each transformation satisfies logical mechanistic consistency and verified precedent rather than relying on superficial diagrammatic compatibility.
9. Schedule aggressive, high-energy, or non-selective processing operations early in the sequence. Strictly defer the introduction of delicate or reactive peripheral features until after all harsh operational conditions have been completed.
10. Preclude competitive cross-interference in multi-center intermediates by incorporating latent, non-interactive precursor states rather than partially attenuated handles. Activate these latent functionalities only when their participation is required downstream.
11. Establish critical spatial configurations and geometric orientations as early as feasible. Verify that all subsequent operations preserve these configurations, scheduling analytical checkpoints to detect and address deviations before advancing downstream.
12. Deploy transient masking strategies judiciously, weighing step economy against operational reliability. Favor concise, well-precedented protection sequences over direct modifications whenever unmasked pathways introduce significant selectivity risks or compromise component integrity.
13. When intermediates contain multiple identical or closely matched reactive sites that must be manipulated independently, install mutually orthogonal masking profiles prior to core assembly to ensure each site can be addressed selectively.
14. Favor stable, readily handled surrogate components and shelf-stable equivalents over generating transient, highly hazardous, or violently unstable intermediate species in situ, establishing safe and reproducible reaction conditions.
15. Select baseline components that balance accessibility against ease of differentiation. Utilize accessible symmetric or pseudo-symmetric precursors differentiated through predictable sequential operations rather than mandating exotic, unvetted asymmetric precursors.
16. Minimize redundant oxidation-state and activation-level cycles by aligning starting material oxidation levels directly with the target architecture, avoiding unnecessary adjustments unless severe step incompatibilities require an indirect route.
17. Streamline compatible consecutive operations without intermediate isolation to maximize efficiency, but deliberately schedule discrete purification checkpoints at stable stages to purge accumulated impurities and confirm intermediate integrity.
18. Verify the exact positional mapping, spatial consistency, and relative orientation of all interactive handles prior to executing irreversible multi-component assemblies or ring closures, ensuring precursors do not assemble into incorrect structural topologies.
